Photo Club Hub

What might come next

Photo Club Hub shows the photographs that members of photography clubs choose to show. Several clubs in one place, so a visitor does not have to find each club's website and learn a new way round it every time. A companion program turns the same information into ordinary web pages a club can put on its own site.

The list below is what might be built next — possibilities, honestly assessed, not promises. Nobody is paid for any of this, so nothing here is a schedule. What actually gets built depends mostly on what people say they want, and on how many clubs decide to take part.

The one thing worth knowing before reading it: the app already knows about far more clubs than have supplied any photographs. Almost every club listed has an empty page waiting for it. That shapes the whole list — a good deal of what follows is about making it easier for a club to fill that page in.

How to reply

Pick the three you would most like to see, and send back their numbers in order of preference — for example 4, 9, 1. The order matters: your first choice counts for more than your third when the answers are added up.

Three rather than five because people are usually sure about their first two or three and guessing after that. Just reply to the email that brought you here.

__GROUPS__

Two more questions, both optional

Is something missing? These are twelve things somebody already thought of. If what you actually want is not among them, saying so is worth more than any ranking, because no amount of ranking can reveal it.

You decide how much weight it carries. If it belongs in your three, put it there — reply with something like “first: being able to print a club’s photos, which is not listed, then 4, 9”. Something worth displacing one of the twelve is exactly what needs hearing. If it is more of a nice-to-have, just mention it and leave your three as they are.

Anything else at all? Including “this will not work, because…”. Doubts about the whole idea, or about how it is being approached, are worth more than encouragement and are much harder to come by. Nobody minds being told the plan is wrong while it is still cheap to change.

Written by one person who builds this in his own time. Nothing here is a promise, and the order things get built in will owe a lot to what comes back from these replies.
